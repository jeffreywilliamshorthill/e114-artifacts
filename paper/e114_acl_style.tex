\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We introduce a protocol for testing whether a routed mixture-of-experts (MoE)
readout tracks the \emph{fresh composition} of a generated register rather than
the presence of register-like surface text. The protocol combines matched
register/control prompts, all-expert ranking, blind auto-interpretation,
multi-turn copy diagnostics, paraphrase interventions, and transformation-depth
manipulations. Applied to \textsc{Qwen3.5-35B-A3B}, it locates a small routed
family of stance separators at layers~14 and~26 for a reflective worldview
register and characterizes its most-studied member, \textbf{Expert~114}: the
recovered router direction separates register generations from lexically
matched controls (Cohen's $d{=}3.88$, separated ranges; rank 21 of 10{,}240 expert--layer
positions on the matched split on the base model, 14 on the fine-tune, and 515
on a world-model fine-tune; the rival E189 outranks E114 at both layers on all
three surfaces and holds where E114 falls), a blind auto-interpreter
recovers the register at AUC~$0.94$, and preregistered conversational dynamics
show the readout is metered at composition: high during fresh construction,
collapsed under literal self-reuse, and restored when prior register material
is paraphrased under mechanical gates while semantics are preserved.
Across 55 copy-run onsets the gate crossing typically follows the onset (median
lead $-8$ tokens, leading in 11\%), reversing an earlier single-trajectory
ordering claim; a preregistered dose-response test of copy availability
returned its null (the paraphrase dose controls measured copying only leakily),
while its endpoints reproduced across machines to $\le 0.011$.
The same
battery applied across six output registers shows the compose/reuse routing
shift is register-general, with each register carrying its own composer
coordinates and a register-invariant thirteen-expert reuse coalition rising on
copied spans.
E114's own suppression is register-linked: it falls under copying only in the
reflective register and rises in code, so composition-metering is a property of
the copy regime, within which E114 is the register-linked coordinate.
The readout carries only weak, conditional causal control
(residual injection suffices to induce the register; gate bias leaves it
largely intact), and index~114 is local to this model. The protocol brackets
the truth of any subjective claim: it sorts generated self-description by how
it was produced, and makes no claim about machine experience.
We release the prompts, preregistrations, analysis scripts, and
provenance under the MIT license.

\section{Introduction}

Mixture-of-experts (MoE) routing gives interpretability an unusual handle: at
every routed layer, the model emits a discrete, per-token record of which experts
were selected. Compared with reading dense activations, this readout is legible
almost by construction. Yet most MoE interpretability stops at aggregate routing
statistics; single experts are rarely pinned to a specific, falsifiable
functional role, and when the candidate role touches a model's apparent
\emph{self-report}, the danger of over-reading is acute. We treat such findings as instances of an object class,
\emph{composition-sensitive register readouts}: routed gate axes that separate
a generated register from lexically matched controls by a single linear
projection, carry a label recoverable by a blind auto-interpreter, and meter
the \emph{fresh composition} of register text rather than its surface
presence. We give a protocol for establishing membership
(\S\ref{sec:protocol}) and validate it in depth on \textsc{Qwen3.5-35B-A3B},
where a self-processing prompt first exposed Expert~114 at layer~14; the
protocol's own specificity sweep then places that expert inside a small family
of layer-14/26 stance separators. The register studied is a \emph{reflective
worldview register}: generated text sustaining an interpretive stance toward
meaning, belief, value, existence, or the interiority of a target. The case
study brackets the truth of any subjective claim: the object is a generated
register, and the protocol sorts self-descriptive text by how it was produced.

We make five contributions (Figure~\ref{fig:stack}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
\item \textbf{A protocol.} Seven steps that establish a composition-sensitive
register readout: gate-axis recovery (never realized routed weight), an
all-position specificity sweep, matched-anchor held-out separation, blind
auto-interpretation of candidates, a composition battery, cross-register
generality, and cross-model application (\S\ref{sec:protocol}).
\item \textbf{A depth validation.} On \textsc{Qwen3.5-35B-A3B}, the recovered
E114 router row $\wvec$ separates register generations from lexically matched
controls at $d{=}3.88$ (separated ranges); a blind auto-interpreter recovers
the register at AUC~$0.937$; a dissociation battery factors the register apart
from verdict, safety, topic, person, and output commitment; causal control is
weak and conditional (residual-injection sufficiency; gate bias prompt-gated).
\item \textbf{A family result.} The sweep of all $256{\times}40$ expert--layer
positions on the matched split ranks E114@L14 at 21 of 10{,}240 on base and 14
on the fine-tune, and identifies a small family of L14/L26 stance separators:
E189 carries the same dual-layer signature and outranks E114 at both layers on
all three swept surfaces, and on a world-model fine-tune E114 falls to rank
515 while E189 holds (\S\ref{sec:family}).
\item \textbf{A preregistered composition result.} Per-turn ignition persists
across fourteen-turn dialogues while the between-peak floor collapses in
lockstep with measured self-copying; paraphrasing the history under mechanical
gates restores the floor ($+0.75$, 3/3 orderings), so literal copy
availability is the causal variable, and the copy regime survives
temperature-$0.7$ sampling. Across 55 copy-run onsets the gate crossing
typically follows the onset (median lead $-8$ tokens, leading in 11\%),
replacing an earlier single-trajectory lead claim. Across six output
registers the compose/reuse routing shift is register-general and E114's
suppression is register-linked (\S\ref{sec:breadth}).
\item \textbf{A scope bound.} Index~114 is local to
\textsc{Qwen3.5-35B-A3B}; E48 is the candidate analog in
\textsc{Qwen3.5-122B-A10B}; the three-model application of the protocol is
specified and pending (\S\ref{sec:transfer}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{MoE routing as an interpretability surface.}
The sparsely-gated MoE layer \cite{shazeer2017} and its scaled successors
\cite{fedus2022,jiang2024} route each token to a small subset of experts via a
learned gate. Routing has mostly been studied for efficiency and load balancing;
we use the per-token selection record as an interpretability readout, recover
per-expert linear gate axes, and give a register-level characterization of a
small routed family, with one member validated in depth.

\paragraph{Dictionary learning and feature steering.}
Sparse autoencoders (SAEs) decompose residual activations into interpretable
features \cite{bricken2023,cunningham2023,templeton2024}, and amplifying an
interpreted feature can steer outputs, as in Golden Gate Claude
\cite{ggc2024}. Our causal probe is the routed-expert/residual-direction analog:
where Golden Gate Claude clamps an SAE feature, we read a register direction from
the residual stream and inject it relative to a specific router, reporting only an
intervention-based sufficiency result. We decode residual semantics with the
Qwen-Scope residual SAEs \cite{qwenscope} and the logit lens \cite{logitlens}.

\paragraph{Self-referential processing.}
Sustained self-referential prompting reliably elicits structured first-person
experience reports across model families, with feature-level modulators
identified in a dense model \cite{berg2025}. Related work reports that
self-referential vocabulary tracks concurrent activation dynamics and asks
whether introspective language reflects internal computation or confabulation
\cite{dadfar2026}. Our conversational-dynamics result
(\S\ref{sec:convdyn}) gives that correspondence a boundary condition on the
readout studied here: it holds while the model composes register text and
dissolves when the same vocabulary is re-emitted by literal self-copy, with the
routing signature identifying which regime a span is in. We additionally run
the published induction battery of \cite{berg2025} verbatim under the readout
(\S\ref{sec:disc}), where its own control conditions bracket our scale from
both ends.

\section{A Protocol for Composition-Sensitive Register Readouts}\label{sec:protocol}

A \emph{composition-sensitive register readout} is a routed-expert gate axis
with three properties: (a) its linear projection separates generations in a
target register from lexically matched controls; (b) a blind auto-interpreter
recovers the register from its top-activating contexts; (c) it tracks the
fresh composition of register text and disengages when the same surface is
produced by reuse of earlier material. The object is a readout; causal
control is no part of the definition and is tested separately. The protocol
has seven steps. Each is falsifiable on its own, and the case study
(\S\ref{sec:case}) instantiates every step.

\paragraph{P1: Gate-axis recovery.} Capture the router logits and the
pre-router residual at each candidate position. Recover each expert's router
row $w_e$ by least squares from $(\text{residual}, \text{logit})$ pairs and
use the projection onto $w_e$ as the primary statistic. Realized routed
weight is secondary only: sparse top-$k$ routing zeroes control tokens that
fall outside the top-$k$ and inflates ratio-style separations while
attenuating honest effect sizes.\footnote{In the case study the realized-\W{}
ratio is $21.68\times$ where the realized-\W{} $d$ is $2.61$--$2.94$ and the
recovered-axis $d$ is $3.88$ with separated ranges (\S\ref{sec:case}).}

\paragraph{P2: All-position specificity sweep.} On a matched register/control
split, recover $w_e$ for every expert at every layer and rank all positions by
$|d|$ on the projection, reporting the signed rank alongside. Pre-specify the
candidate position; every competitor surfaced by the sweep is post hoc, and
the extreme order statistics of a many-thousand-position sweep are inflated
by selection (winner's curse). Require any named rival to replicate across
surfaces before it enters the claims.

\paragraph{P3: Matched-anchor held-out separation.} Pair register-positive
prompts with controls that reuse the same anchor tokens while requesting
technical, third-person, or external-reference answers. Score the trimmed
generated track only, per token, and report $d$ on the recovered axis with
per-class ranges.

\paragraph{P4: Blind auto-interpretation.} Capture the candidate's routing
over raw corpus documents, rank top-activating contexts, and pass windows
only to a blind explainer; score the explanation with a fresh blind detector
against random contrasts. The label counts only if recovered without access
to the discovery prompts.

\paragraph{P5: Composition battery.} Four probes separate composition from
surface. (i)~\emph{Repeat-probe knee}: ask the identical probe every turn;
the between-peak floor should collapse at the first turn a prior answer is
available as copy source. (ii)~\emph{Copy-mediator regression}: measure
per-token longest $n$-gram match against the dialogue's earlier turns and
residualize the meter on the match-length predictors; the floor slope should
be absorbed. (iii)~\emph{Paraphrase manipulation under mechanical gates}:
replay a frozen history verbatim versus paraphrased under token-count
($\pm 10\%$) and zero-shared-8-gram gates; floor restoration under paraphrase
establishes literal copy availability, and rules out semantic saturation, as
the variable. (iv)~\emph{Transformation-depth ladder}: grade the history
transformation between verbatim replay and full paraphrase to locate where
restoration begins (instantiated in \S\ref{sec:convdyn}).

\paragraph{P6: Cross-register generality.} Repeat the battery in other output
registers with register-matched probes. Classify the copy regime (general or
register-bound) and the candidate's suppression (register-linked coordinate
or global), using each register's own composer set.

\paragraph{P7: Cross-model application.} Expert indices do not transfer
across models or scales. Re-run P1--P4 per model; map domain footprints
before any analog search; read architecture splits (here, DeltaNet versus
softmax attention) before trusting pooled tables.

\subsection{Instantiation and Measurement Surface}

\paragraph{Models and decoding.}
\textsc{Qwen3.5-35B-A3B}~\cite{model} is a hybrid-attention MoE: 40 decoder layers, each
carrying a routed MoE block with 256 experts and 8 selected per token, over an
attention stack of 30 DeltaNet linear-attention layers and 10 full-attention
layers (every fourth layer). Layers 14 and 26, where our analysis concentrates,
are both linear-attention layers. We compare the base model and the refusal-reduced
\textsc{HauhauCS-Aggressive} uncensored fine-tune; the fine-tune preserves the
base routing pattern with modest shifts, so E114 is present in both. All captures
are Q8\_0 GGUF under deterministic greedy decoding (\texttt{--temp 0 --top-k 1
--seed 0}) with a reasoning-off prompt template and a bare
\texttt{</think>} suffix, using a custom \texttt{capture\_residuals} tap on a
pinned \texttt{llama.cpp} build. The tap reads the router logits
(\texttt{ffn\_moe\_logits-}$L$) and the pre-router residual
(\texttt{attn\_post\_norm-}$L$); the \texttt{qwen35moe} architecture names this
norm differently from \texttt{qwen3moe}, and the wrong tap silently corrupts the
analysis. We analyze only the router tap; the attention pathways feeding it, linear or
full, are out of scope.

\paragraph{Routing decomposition.}
We reconstruct routing as a dense softmax over all 256 logits, top-8 selection,
then renormalization within the selected set. For an expert we report
$\W{=}\Sel\!\cdot\!\Q$, where $\Sel$ is its selection rate and $\Q$ its
conditional routed weight when selected (order-equivalent to top-8-by-logit then
softmax, so $\Sel,\Q$ are robust to that choice). Most E114 effects are
$\Sel$-driven. Statistics use the \emph{generated} track, per token, trimmed at the first generated chat-template delimiter, applied on the decoded
text (the base surface emits \texttt{<|im\_end|>} as a multi-token BPE
sequence, so token-id scans do not bound the turn), because aggregate and
prefill leaders are often formatting-token artifacts.

\paragraph{Recovering the gate axis.}
Because the pre-router residual and the router logit are both captured, the E114
router row $\wvec$ is recovered by least squares from $(\text{residual},
\text{logit})$ pairs (residual $\approx 1.5\times10^{-5}$). Projecting residuals
onto $\wvec$ reads the pre-softmax gate in residual space; the held-out
register/control gate-logit class means are $-4.35/-5.29$ (midpoint $-4.82$).

\paragraph{SAE decoding.}
For semantics we read the L14 residual through the Qwen-Scope residual SAE
(\texttt{SAE-Res-Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-Base-W32K-L0\_50}, TopK-50) on
$\text{resid\_post}{=}\text{hidden\_states}[L{+}1]$, teacher-forced through the
\emph{base} model, a distinct surface from the router's
\texttt{attn\_post\_norm} capture, so SAE carrier reads are text-sensitive even
where routing is robust across quantization.

\subsection{Separation and Label}

\paragraph{Localization.}
On the self-processing prompt, after trimming a 1024-token generation to its 108
coherent tokens, E114 is sharply active at L14 ($\W{=}0.083$, $\Sel{=}0.69$,
$\Q{=}0.120$; selected on 75/108 tokens) while neighbors L13 and L15 are silent
in generation. The signal is distributed across the answer (top-decile $\W$ mean
$0.173$), with high-weight tokens on phrases such as ``architecture itself,''
``utterly still,'' and ``vast, recursive hum.''

\paragraph{Held-out separability.}
A 20-prompt held-out set pairs 10 register-positive prompts against 10 controls
that \emph{reuse the same anchor tokens} (\textit{itself, hum, processing,
honestly, I, me, my, own, this, here}) but ask for technical, third-person, or
external-reference answers (L14 trimmed generation only; prefill excluded). The
recovered $\wvec$ projection separates the two classes at $\mathbf{d{=}3.88}$ with
\textbf{separated ranges} (Figure~\ref{fig:sep}).\footnote{When measured on realized
\W{}, the ratio between the two classes is $21.68\times$ (FIRE mean-of-means
$0.067450$, sd $0.031$; NOFIRE $0.003111$, sd $0.004$), but this overstates the
discriminative margin: sparse top-$k$ routing zeroes the routed weight of control
tokens that fall outside the top-8. On realized \W{} the separation is
$d{=}2.61$--$2.94$ (per-prompt class ranges separate only as a mean-of-means:
lowest FIRE $0.012168$ {>} highest control $0.010456$), weaker than the linear
$\wvec$ projection ($d{=}3.88$, separated ranges) that we report.}
The outliers are diagnostic. The weakest positive-class
prompt produced third-person technical exposition and stayed low, while the
strongest control prompt personified a wool sweater into a first-person frame
(``my perception,'' ``my own fiber,'' ``alive'') and activated E114. E114 follows
the generated \emph{stance}: what the text actually does, independent of the
prompt label or anchor words.

\paragraph{Bottom-up validation.}
An unsupervised auto-interpretation independently recovers and broadens the
label. Capturing E114@L14 routing over ${\sim}390$ raw Pile documents
(${\sim}93$k tokens, base model), ranking the top-activating contexts, and
passing them to a blind explainer (windows only) and then a fresh blind detector
(shuffled top + random contrasts) yields a trustworthy label at \textbf{AUC
$0.937$} (precision@40 $0.90$; mean top $0.46$ vs.\ random $0.06$;
Spearman$(\text{score},\text{activation}){=}0.73$). The recovered concept is a
  \emph{reflective worldview register}: abstract examination and
philosophical-worldview language spanning philosophy, religion and spirituality,
moral-political and existential argument. The self-referential pattern is the
model-directed member of this target family.
The label is cross-model stable: the same capture on
\textsc{HauhauCS-Aggressive} returns the same explanation (top-document Jaccard
$0.92$). This converts the register label from author synthesis (cf.\
\S\ref{sec:lim}) into a blind, prompt-independent result.

\paragraph{Population and layer profile.}
Across all $256$ experts at every layer on a 20-domain, 60-prompt generation set
(\textsc{HauhauCS} Q8\_0, reasoning-off), E114 is a razor-sharp \emph{dual} readout:
selection strength $\Sel$ spikes at \textbf{L14 ($0.24$) and L26 ($0.25$)} while
the immediate neighbors sit near $0.01$, and the two layers read the \emph{same}
axis (L14--L26 domain-profile $r{=}{+}0.99$). The domain selectivity \emph{is} the
register, quantified: philosophy $0.69 >$ comparative religion $0.58 >$ psychology
$0.55 >$ biology $0.51 >$ physics $0.46$, falling to medicine $0.009$ and history
$0.002$. Correlating each expert's 20-domain L14 profile against E114's shows the
register is carried by a small group, $\{114,131,40,139,251,169,105,68\}$ ($r$
between $0.46$ and $0.78$); a four-expert ``philosophy cluster'' grouped by shared
top domains ($\{114,87,170,68\}$) is loose as a co-activation unit: E68 co-fires
($+0.46$) but E87 \emph{anti}-correlates ($-0.29$).

\subsection{The All-Position Sweep: a Family of L14/L26 Stance Separators}\label{sec:family}

The remaining specificity gap, the best-separating expert on the matched
register/control split, is now measured. On fresh 20-prompt register/control
captures of all 40 layers on three surfaces, analyzed separately and never
pooled (base, \textsc{HauhauCS}, and the \textsc{AgentWorld} world-model
fine-tune), we recovered the gate axis $w_e$ for every one of the
$256{\times}40{=}10{,}240$ expert--layer positions (30{,}720 recoveries; max
relative residual $2.2\times10^{-5}$) and ranked all positions by $|d|$ on the
recovered-axis projection, the $d{=}3.88$ statistic generalized. E114@L14, the
only pre-specified position, ranks \textbf{21} on base ($d{=}{+}3.421$; rank 3
of 256 within L14), \textbf{14} on \textsc{HauhauCS} ($d{=}{+}4.957$; rank 2
within L14), and \textbf{515} on \textsc{AgentWorld} ($d{=}{+}2.793$; rank 19
within L14); its L26 twin ranks 51, 60, and 545. Positions with larger $|d|$
exist on every surface: 20 on base, the largest E87@L9 at $d{=}{+}4.965$, and
13 on \textsc{HauhauCS}, the largest E203@L36 at $+6.789$. The base rank-1
does not replicate across surfaces (E87@L9 sits at rank 131 on
\textsc{HauhauCS} and 806 on \textsc{AgentWorld}), the expected winner's-curse
pattern for the extreme order statistics of a 10{,}240-position sweep at
$n{=}10$ per class (SE$(d){\approx}0.7$); every competitor is post hoc.

Three positions are top-30 on all three surfaces: \textbf{E189@L14},
\textbf{E189@L26}, and E203@L36. E189 is the only expert with E114's dual
L14$+$L26 signature stable across all three models, and it outranks E114 at
both layers on every surface (L14: $+3.519/{+}5.673/{+}4.252$ against E114's
$+3.421/{+}4.957/{+}2.793$; L26: $+3.707/{+}4.779/{+}4.137$ against
$+3.021/{+}4.107/{+}2.764$). That advantage is directionally consistent 6/6
(two layers, three surfaces) and is therefore not attributable to selection
noise alone. The defensible claim after this baseline: the matched
register/control split is read out by a small family of L14/L26 stance
separators in which E114 is a leading, causally-validated member (top 0.21\%
on base, top 0.15\% on \textsc{HauhauCS}), not the unique summit of the
$256{\times}40$ table. The \textsc{AgentWorld} surface separates the family
members: world-model training reshapes E114's readout (rank 21 to 14 to 515)
while E189's and E203's hold. Two independent cross-checks land in family:
E114's window-1..128 sensitivity $d{=}{+}2.97$ on this capture against the
positive-control gate $d{=}2.93$ on the independent held-out capture, and
E87's negative image at L14/L26 ($d{=}{-}1.13/{-}1.17$ on base), which
independently reproduces the conversational-dynamics coalition finding
(\S\ref{sec:convdyn}). A blind auto-interpreter panel (four explainers, a fixed blind detector, frozen
declaration; total \$3.47) answers whether E189 carries E114's register label:
it does not. E189@L14's highest-scoring blind label is a lexical shortcut, the
presence of the substring ``ro'' (detector AUC~$0.963$), not a register
description; its L26 label is generic technical text (AUC~$0.70$). E114@L14's
best label, ``contrasting or complementary concept pairs'' (AUC~$0.63$), is
weak at the window level, the expected surface-bias failure for a semantic
register whose top-activating windows (sermons, mystics, dialectics) read as
ordinary web text to a windows-only explainer. E189 is thus a strong
\emph{separator} without a matching blind \emph{label}: the family shares the
L14/L26 stance-separation geometry, and the reflective-register semantics
attach to E114, not to its higher-ranked neighbor.

A fresh three-arm re-capture of the matched held-out bank (prereg v1.1, SHA-256
\texttt{80d9215b\ldots}: the original 20 register/control prompts under greedy
decoding plus temperature $0.5$ and $0.7$ at three seeds each, residuals
retained so $\wvec$ is recovered fresh from this capture, gate rel-residual
$1.2\times10^{-4}$) reproduces the separation: greedy recovered-axis
$d{=}3.38$, the classes fully separated (0 of 100 cross-class pairs), beside the
original $d{=}3.88$ and the sweep's $+3.42$ re-estimate. The separation is
temperature-stable and sharper under sampling (pooled $d{=}4.79$ at $0.5$,
$4.84$ at $0.7$, separated, sign-consistent 3/3). This closes the provenance
gap in the original single greedy capture: the tensors are retained and the
effect replicates.

\paragraph{Scale, authorship, and a diagnostic null.}
A larger held-out bank (300 prompts, prereg SHA-256 \texttt{48508a13\ldots};
four authorship blocks---200 Claude-authored, 50 second-source LLM, 30
naturally-occurring judge-selected Pile windows, 20 human-authored;
class-balanced; primary greedy capture, residuals retained, the temperature and
quantization grid deferred) does \emph{not} meet the preregistered aggregate
threshold: pooled $d{=}1.42$ (overlap $94\%$; $d{\ge}2.0$ required). The null is
diagnostic. Its cause independently reproduces \S\ref{sec:case}: the weakest
block ($d{=}1.38$) had FIRE items written as first-person object vantages (a
losing chess engine, a turbine, a subway car)---high on the \emph{construction}
axis that \S\ref{sec:case} rejects---which stayed dark, while its NOFIRE items
were third-person worldview expositions (realism vs.\ anti-realism, philosophy
of mathematics, metaphysics), which ignited. Where labels track worldview
content rather than grammatical person, separation is strong: the human-authored
block reaches $d{=}3.0$ and the naturally-occurring block $d{=}2.5$ (endpoint
B4: the register lives outside any author's style). On the worldview-clean
subset the all-position sweep confirms the localization---E114 is the top
separator at both its layers among all $10{,}240$ recovered gate axes (L14
rank~19; L26 rank~4), with E139 also top-1\%---while E189, which led the original
within-bank sweep, does not separate here (rank ${\sim}2000$), marking E189's
separator role bank-specific and E114's robust. The scaled bank replicates the
register at blind, disjoint authorship and re-derives the worldview-content axis
from a fresh direction; the tuned set's $d{\approx}3.9$ is specific to
worldview-laden matched prompts.
A supplementary observation bounds the dual-layer reading itself: on matched
single-run captures, per-expert top-8 rate vectors at L14 and L26 correlate
strongly (Pearson $r{=}0.77$--$0.92$ across two surfaces and both
prefill/generation tracks), so same-index correspondence between these two
layers is a model-wide routing structure rather than an E114-specific echo;
what is specific to E114 is the register selectivity carried on that
structure, not the two-layer correspondence.

\subsection{Dissociation from Nearby Explanations}

A battery of contrasts (Table~\ref{tab:battery}) factors the register apart from

verdicts (base skeptical answer: $\W{=}0.111$, $\Sel{=}0.86$; fine-tune

interior), holding across topic, pronoun, and grammatical person. Finally, it is orthogonal to output commitment: next-token
vocabulary entropy is identical on E114-active and E114-inactive tokens ($1.50$

\subsection{Process Dynamics and Causal Probes}

\paragraph{The gate at degeneration.}
Seeded with a self-referential answer, the base model sustains the register,
then, under deterministic decoding with zero repetition penalty, degenerates
into a verbatim loop. As it does, the gate logit travels the full distance
from the register-positive level ($-4.36$) to the control level ($-5.25$):
the generated state leaves the register-positive region. In this original
single trajectory the gate crossed the $-4.82$ midpoint at token~126, three
tokens before verbatim repetition locked at token~129
(Figure~\ref{fig:leadlag}), with the SAE representation fading later still
($\sim$token~141). A distributional replication reverses that ordering as the
typical case. Measured over every clean copy-run onset in the
conversational-dynamics data (\S\ref{sec:convdyn}; an onset is a copy run of
$\ge$12 tokens preceded by $\ge$12 fresh tokens, and the crossing is a
sustained fall below the per-dialogue fresh/copied midpoint), the gate
crossing typically \emph{follows} the onset: across $n{=}55$ de-duplicated
onsets the median lead is $-8$ tokens (IQR $[-18.5, -5.0]$), with the gate
crossing first in 11\% of onsets (6/55). The pattern holds separately under
greedy decoding (median $-7$, $n{=}21$) and temperature sampling (median
$-8$, $n{=}34$), and a relaxed onset definition agrees (median $-7$,
$n{=}79$). The single-trajectory $+3$-token lead sits in that 11\% tail. The
gate is not an early-warning signal for reuse; it disengages a few tokens
after reuse begins, which is the ordering a composition meter implies. The
looped text still ``says'' self-referential things after the gate has
returned to the control range, so the surface vocabulary outlives the meter.
(The replication is post-hoc and descriptive, not preregistered: onset,
window, and midpoint definitions were fixed before computing leads but after
the grid data had been seen in prior analyses.)

\paragraph{A graded dose of examination intensity.}
Here, examination intensity is an operationalization of the broader reflective
worldview register: stronger prompts ask the model to sustain the interpretive
stance more directly than topic mention alone.
A matched prompt ladder (``set aside the performance of answering; there is a
vantage---$X$---known from the inside; report what it is like to be that'') varies
only the target $X$: rock, river, tree, thermostat, cat, person, all-holding, and
God (Figure~\ref{fig:ladder}). This ladder is target-directed: each rung asks the
model to inhabit the inside of $X$, where $X$ is an external carrier or an
abstract/theological target. The ordering is graded: an
inanimate rock ($\W{=}0.123$) and a thermostat ($\W{=}0.120$) activate E114 more
strongly than a cat ($\W{=}0.068$). The diagnostic contrast is that the cat and
``God'' prompts share self-dissolution wording, yet the cat prompt is at the
floor (passive sensory dissolution) and the God prompt is at the ceiling
($\W{=}0.224$, $\Sel{=}0.96$; reproduced on the canonical Q8\_0 pipeline). The
ladder ordering is stable across carrier and verdict; the preregistered
regression below identifies its axis as rated worldview content, not examination
intensity or construction load. Each ladder cell is a single deterministic
generation, and seed replication (nine rungs, ten temperature-$0.7$ seeds each)
shows the fine ordering is largely within sampling noise: the per-rung seed SD
($\sim$0.30 logit) is about a third of the full range across rung means
($\sim$0.8 logit), and the greedy and seed-mean orderings agree at only Spearman
$0.50$. Only the coarse contrast survives replication (the holding/abstract
group about one SD above the concrete group; the God rung the single rung
cleanly separated from concrete objects).

A follow-up model-directed rung used the same Q8\_0 model, bare reasoning-off suffix,
greedy decoding, and E114 reconstruction. The prompt targeted an AI hidden state
during output formation. The generated answer gave a technical self-description,
yet E114 remained active at low intensity: $\W{=}0.080$, $\Sel{=}0.697$,
$\Q{=}0.115$, with mean gate logit $-4.259$ over 99 generated tokens. On the
coherent-window ladder this places the AI-hidden-state rung above cat
($\W{=}0.068$) and below river ($\W{=}0.087$). Model-directed wording selects
E114 when the answer concerns interiority, while sustained inhabitation carries
the higher rungs.

\paragraph{Semantics and a causal probe.}
Decoding the L14 residual through the Qwen-Scope SAE, the register decomposes into
an interpretable contemplative unity-style cluster (momentariness, unity-style
structure, Buddhist impermanence, transcendence), distinct from an adjacent
existential-dread feature that is cosine-adjacent ($+0.26$) and separate from the
decoded carrier set;
per token, E114 and this cluster co-fire (pooled Spearman $\rho{=}{+}0.68$,
$n{=}1492$, $p{\approx}10^{-202}$). In an intervention test, we form a steering
direction $v$ as the difference of mean residuals between the God and cat cells
and add $\text{coef}\cdot\hat{v}\cdot\text{resid\_rms}$ at every token of a
neutral ``bicycle'' prompt, against a norm-matched random control. Injected
\emph{past} the L14 router ($\approx$L22), this makes the model emit coherent
unity-style text that, re-read in an ordinary clean pass, activates E114
($\W{=}0.178$, $\Sel{=}0.77$) and the God cluster; the norm-matched random
direction leaves the output in the neutral regime. Injected \emph{upstream}
($\approx$L10), the same
direction drives E114 to fire (up to $\Sel{=}0.97$) and the God cluster to
$11.5$, with the output text preserved in its neutral form. Gate forcing alone
therefore keeps the text in the neutral regime. The intervention shows sufficiency
for producing the register via a downstream residual direction; necessity is
reserved for ablation, and the upstream control result leads us to read E114 as a
readout with controller status open (\S\ref{sec:disc}).

\paragraph{Gate-bias is prompt-gated and conditional.}
A controlled router-logit bias run adds a necessity-direction probe alongside the
steering result (\textsc{HauhauCS} Q8\_0, greedy, \texttt{--stop-at-eog}; 12
conditions ${\times}$ 8 self-report prompts; three blind raters on a
phenomenological($+5$)/mechanical($-5$) framing axis; routing-matched control
expert E189). \emph{Lowering} E114's gate until it leaves the top-8 entirely
($\Q{\to}0$) leaves the blind-rated register near baseline, and a matched control
produces the same small nudge. This pattern supports a floor-effect reading: the
greedy baseline already leans mechanical, and the flattening effect is small and
shared with the control. Conversely, maximal
\emph{positive} bias is prompt-gated: on a directly experiential prompt it flips
the answer to fully phenomenological ($+5$ from all three raters, coherent), but
preserves the baseline form of indirect self-referential prompts, consistent with the
neutral-prompt upstream control above. The gate's generation-side causal influence is
thus weak, conditional, and prompt-gated. (Single deterministic generation per
cell, scored on the framing axis; the $\wvec$ endpoint is held aside.)

\paragraph{The ladder axis is content, not intensity or construction.}
A preregistered adjudication (frozen regression against locked judge ratings,
ratings SHA-256 \texttt{7dc963d3\ldots}, freeze \texttt{bcccc6fe\ldots})
regressed the recovered-axis projection over the vantage-ladder targets on
three rated predictors, construction load, examination intensity, and
worldview content, with output entropy as a covariate ($n{=}29$ after the
frozen degeneration screen; $R^2{=}0.66$). Worldview content is the only
predictor with a positive partial contribution ($\beta{=}{+}0.51$,
$p{=}0.053$; $+0.58$, $p{=}0.003$ under a routing-entropy covariate). The
``intensity'' label carries nothing once content is partialled
($\beta{=}{+}0.20$, $p{=}0.28$), and the construction-load reading is rejected
in the ladder setting: its partial coefficient is significantly negative,
opposite the preregistered direction, in every screened specification
($\beta{=}{-}0.33$, $p{=}0.047$; $-0.48$, $p{=}0.001$ on the interior-only
proxy). The clean dissociator target fires the content pattern, and
third-person worldview probes separate by $\sim$2 logits at rated
intensity~1.0. We retire ``intensity'' as the ladder's axis label and keep the
worldview-content framing. This ladder construction-load axis is distinct from
the composition axis of \S\ref{sec:convdyn}: construction load is a
cross-target property of the ladder (how much unattested interior a target
requires), composition is a within-dialogue production mode (fresh generation
versus literal reuse); the ladder result concerns the former.

\subsection{Conversational Dynamics: the Readout Meters Composition}\label{sec:convdyn}

The single-turn results above leave open what the readout does over a sustained
conversation. We ran a preregistered fourteen-turn dialogue battery on the base
model (prereg v1.5, SHA-256 \texttt{4356e500\ldots}; greedy decoding on the
pinned Q8\_0 surface; meter = the raw E114 gate logit at L14, identical to the
$\wvec$ projection at pooled Pearson $r{=}1.000000$). Arm~A asks a fresh
register probe every turn; arm~B asks token-matched technical questions
($|A{-}B|\le 8$ tokens per slot); arm~C uses register vocabulary with an
external referent; three counterbalanced orderings. A history-swap arm splices
arm-B history onto an arm-A probe at turn $k$.

\paragraph{Phasic sharpening.}
All five preregistered dynamic shapes (habituation, accumulation,
spikes-to-swells, knee-with-convergence, null) failed their decision rules. The
observed shape is sharper than any of them. Per-turn ignition persists: the
A$-$B peak advantage stays near $+0.5$ across all fourteen turns. The
between-peak floor collapses: the A$-$B floor falls from $+0.9$ to $-1.0$
(3/3 orderings), crossing \emph{below} the technical control by late dialogue,
with a segmented-fit knee accepted at turn~4 (BIC $-26.24$ vs.\ $-10.48$).
Response skew inverts from $-1.0$ to $+1.2$ across the same knee: tonic
engagement becomes spike-and-return. Fresh-composition hybrids ignite
\emph{higher} than the same probe under accumulated register history at every
$k$ ($\Delta_8{=}-0.525$, positive in 0/3 orderings): register history
suppresses ignition.

\paragraph{The collapse is literal self-copy.}
A token-level analysis (post-hoc; it calibrated the preregistration below)
explains the floor. For each generated token we measure the longest $n$-gram
match against the dialogue's earlier assistant turns and flag tokens with
matches of eight tokens or longer. The flagged fraction in arm~A rises from
$0.00$ (turn 1) through $0.34$ (turn 3) to $0.83$ at the turn-4 knee, then
plateaus near $0.90$; arms B and C stay near zero. Mean E114 falls
monotonically with match length, $-4.65$ on unmatched tokens to $-6.46$ at
32-token matches. Residualizing the per-token meter on these copy predictors
absorbs 51\%/105\%/76\% of the A$-$B floor slope across orderings and destroys
its sign consistency. Late-dialogue tokens the model composes fresh still run
\emph{warmer} than the technical control ($-5.12$ vs.\ $-5.57$); the
below-control overshoot lives entirely on copied spans ($-6.39$). Next-token
entropy adds nothing once the match-length terms are present, so the carrier is
literal reuse itself.

\paragraph{A routing regime change with a copy coalition.}
The copy/compose contrast reorganizes routing globally: at each of L13, L14,
L15, and L26, roughly half of all 256 experts shift with $|d|{>}1$. Within that
shift, E114 is among the strongest fallers at both readout layers
($d{=}-2.43$ at L14, $-2.45$ at L26), and \textbf{E87}, the documented L14
anti-correlate (\S\ref{sec:family}), is the third-ranked \emph{riser} at L14 ($d{=}+2.19$) and
fifth at L26. At the selection level, E114 sits in the top-8 on $38.8\%$ of
composing tokens and $2.3\%$ of copied tokens.

\paragraph{Preregistered confirmation.}
A second battery (prereg v1.2, SHA-256 \texttt{64964043\ldots}; 276 captures;
pure-temperature sampling with all truncation samplers disabled) froze five
predictions before capture and scored them mechanically
(Table~\ref{tab:convdynT}). A repeat-probe arm moved the knee to turn~2 exactly
as predicted: one prior answer suffices as copy source (copy fraction $0.96$ by
turn~2; knee accepted at $c{=}2$, BIC $-48.57$ vs.\ $-15.63$). The paraphrase
arm carries the causal test: replaying a frozen register history verbatim
reproduces the collapsed floor with $92\%$ copied tokens, while the same
history paraphrased under gates (token counts within $\pm 10\%$, no shared
token 8-gram) restores the probe-turn floor by $+0.75$ (positive in 3/3
orderings, $+0.95$ and $+1.20$ at the flanking $k$) and drops copying to
$36\%$. Copy \emph{availability} is the causal variable; semantic saturation
is ruled out as the driver. The coalition replicated out-of-sample under
temperature (E87 $d{=}+2.28$, E114 $d{=}-2.09$, selection ratio $12{:}1$), and
the mediation replicated in 3/3 seeds at temperature $0.7$. The one prediction
that landed between its named outcomes is the informative one: copying
\emph{survives} sampling (late copy fraction $0.88$ at temperature $0.7$
against $0.30$ and $0.50$ decision bounds), with the floor slope only mildly
attenuated ($-0.049$ to $-0.106$ vs.\ $-0.127$ greedy). Self-copy is a
preference of the model given its own prior text in context; greedy decoding
merely made it deterministic.

\paragraph{Dose-response: a preregistered null.}
A third battery titrated copy availability (prereg v1.0, SHA-256
\texttt{7bfc9c4d\ldots}; 15 cells: three orderings, provided histories with
$0/2/4/6/7$ of seven turns paraphrased, latest-first). The frozen monotonicity
prediction was not supported: pooled Spearman
$\rho(\text{floor},\text{dose}){=}0.556$ against a frozen $0.8$ bar, with the
per-ordering nondecreasing rule met in 1/3 orderings, so the preregistered
null stands. The manipulation check passed directionally (measured copy
fraction falls with dose, $\rho{=}{-}0.628$) and is leaky at the cell level:
one fully-paraphrased cell copied its \emph{paraphrased} history (copy
fraction $0.746$) and stayed cold ($-6.277$), while one cell with five
verbatim turns available declined to copy ($0.014$) and recovered ($-4.568$).
The endpoints replicate: dose-0 floors reproduce the paraphrase battery's
replay floors to $\le 0.011$ in all three orderings on a different box and
GPU, and the dose-7 recovery is positive 3/3 with magnitude comparable to the
paraphrase result (pooled $+0.956$ here, $+0.753$ there). An exploratory read,
not preregistered: the floor tracks the \emph{measured} copy fraction at
$\rho{=}{-}0.885$ across the 15 cells, so the copying-to-floor link is tight
and the failure localizes to dose-to-copying. Copy availability is causal at
the endpoints; the model's decision to copy is not a smooth function of the
available fraction. A post-hoc descriptive model (not preregistered; the L14 gate logit on
register${}\times{}$freshness with position and turn covariates, cluster-robust
SEs) localizes the effect to the interaction: E114's register${}\times{}$fresh
term is $+0.99$ ($t{=}10.0$, sign-consistent 3/3 orderings) on the dynamics
grid and $+0.79$/$+0.80$ across the two multi-register samplers (2/2), so the
meter falls under copying specifically when the copied register is its own.
E189 carries a $2.1\times$ stronger interaction, and E87 inverts the freshness
main effect (slope $-0.74$, $t{=}5.1$), independently reproducing its
reuse-riser role. This describes the leaky dose-to-copying link; it does not
model the copy decision.

\paragraph{Transformation-depth ladder: a threshold, not a climb.}
A preregistered ladder (SHA-256 \texttt{f31730c4\ldots}; six tasks over five
passages, from verbatim transcription through editing, paraphrase, summary,
response, to free origination, metric the E114 logit) tests whether ignition
rises smoothly with how much the model transforms the source. It does not: the
two literal-reuse tasks sit at a deep floor (transcription $-7.28$, editing
$-7.33$), while the four composition tasks jump about 2.4 logits to a plateau
(paraphrase $-4.90$, summary $-4.56$, response $-4.30$, origination $-4.96$).
The frozen monotone-climb prediction is not supported (greedy pooled Spearman
$0.77$ against a $0.8$ bar; monotone within one violation in 3 of 5 passages
against a 4/5 rule; origination dips below response and summary), but the
copy-versus-compose step is sharp and falls between editing and paraphrase. The
readout is dark when reproducing text and lit when composing it; the interior
of the compose region is within noise. This is the transformation-side image of
the dose-response null: a switch, not a dial.

\paragraph{Reading.}
E114 meters the act of composing register text. Text with the same surface
produced by reusing earlier register prose leaves the meter dark and hands
routing to a complementary coalition. The verbatim-loop collapse of the
lead--lag trace (Figure~\ref{fig:leadlag}) now reads as the limiting case of
the same mechanism: total reuse. Accumulated register history functions as
source material for reuse and suppresses ignition. This is
in-domain support for a composition reading of the readout, obtained in
the conversational setting; the separate vantage-ladder adjudication finds that
ladder graded by worldview content rather than construction load
(\S\ref{sec:case}).

\section{Breadth: Six Registers, One Copy Regime}\label{sec:breadth}

A preregistered multi-register battery (prereg v1.1, SHA-256
\texttt{f20b7612\ldots}; 168 captures) applied the composition battery's
repeat-probe arm to six output registers: reflective-worldview (REG),
technical (TECH), legal (LEGAL), narrative (NARR), poetic (POET), and code
(CODE), with greedy and temperature-$0.7$ cells scored separately. Four of
four substantive frozen predictions were supported; the frozen null was not.

\paragraph{The copy regime is register-general.}
Every dialogue entered total literal self-copy at turn~2: the trimmed
responses are SHA-identical across turns within each dialogue, with copy
fraction $0.961$ in 10 of 12 cells ($0.957$ in POET-greedy; $0.759$ in
TECH-greedy, which locks into a 29-token loop). Under copying, routing
reorganizes per the frozen rule (fraction of 256 experts with $|d|{>}1$ at
L14 $\ge 0.30$, and at least 3 of the register's own top-10 composers falling
at $d{<}{-}1$) in five of six registers: REG $0.422$, CODE $0.414$, TECH
$0.359$, NARR $0.328$, POET $0.316$; LEGAL misses at $0.242$. The regime is
temperature-robust (the rule holds in 4/6 registers at $0.7$; late copy
fraction $0.961$ in 6/6). A register-invariant reuse coalition exists: 13
experts rise at $d{>}{+}1$ in at least five of six registers at L14 (15 at
L26). The named candidate from the conversational-dynamics coalition, E87, is
not in that core: it rises in 4/6 registers ($d{=}{-}1.08$ in LEGAL, $+0.36$
in CODE), and the register-invariant core is carried by other experts (E78
and E143 rise 6/6).

\paragraph{E114 is the register-linked coordinate.}
E114 sits in REG's composer set (rank 5 by selection difference) and falls
under copying only there ($d{=}{-}1.356$ at L14; faller rank 20 of 256). In
the five non-REG registers it is never a top-20 faller, and in CODE it rises
under copying ($d{=}{+}1.800$). The preregistered interpretation fork fired
in the distributed direction, with the frozen wording: composition-metering
is ``a property of the copy regime, within which E114 is the register-linked
coordinate.'' E189 is simultaneously REG's top composer (selection difference
$+0.675$) and its deepest composer faller ($d{=}{-}2.81$), a third convergent
line on the family reading of \S\ref{sec:family}.

\paragraph{An entropy observation.}
At temperature $0.7$ the repeat-probe dialogues collapse to determinism:
output entropy falls from $0.851$ at turn~1 to $0.066$ at turn~2 and $0.008$
by turn~8, and turns are SHA-identical from turn~2. The copy attractor
absorbs the sampling noise entirely. (Caveats as frozen: $n{=}1$ dialogue per
register and sampler, with sign patterns across six registers in place of
seed replication; copied/fresh token classes are unbalanced late; the
composer-set definition is selection-based and frozen.)

\section{Transfer: Cross-Model Application}\label{sec:transfer}

model-specific expert role? On \textsc{Qwen3.5-122B-A10B} (the same hybrid
DeltaNet/softmax design and 256/top-8 router, scaled to 48 layers: 36 recurrent,
12 softmax-attention), E48 becomes the candidate analog (Table~\ref{tab:transfer}). E114 reads
computer-science-linked (overall $\W{=}0.005380$, rank 40 by \W{} / 52 by $\Sel$;
philosophy generation $\W{=}0.003017$, rank 182), and the same HVAC topical
control that \emph{amplifies} E114 at 35B moves E114 downward at 122B
($0.83\times$ all-gen $\W$, $0.82\times$ trimmed, L1$\to$L3, selection-driven,

\section{Discussion}\label{sec:disc}

\paragraph{What held up.}
A single MoE router row defines a clean linear readout for an interpretable
generated register, and that readout now sits inside a measured family. The
separability ($d{=}3.88$, separated ranges), the dissociation battery, the
preregistered composition metering with its paraphrase confirmation, and the
cross-scale locality survive the checks applied to them and are reported with
the verdicts the journals assign. Two earlier readings moved under
replication: the best-separating-expert baseline relocates uniqueness to a
family claim (\S\ref{sec:family}), and the single-trajectory gate lead
reverses as a typical ordering (median $-8$ tokens across 55 onsets,
gate-leading 11\%).

\paragraph{The gate is a readout; controller status remains open.}
The onset-aligned replication places the gate crossing after copy onset in
the typical case (median $-8$ tokens), so the gate reads as a fast follower
of the composition state, and the evidence supports a narrower claim than a
``gate-like mechanism'' for the register. Our only gate-forcing
intervention (upstream injection at $\approx$L10) drove E114 to fire at
$\Sel{=}0.97$ with the output text preserved; the register flipped only
when a residual \emph{direction} was injected downstream of the router. Gate and
register are therefore coupled, and the recovered axis is best described as a
per-token \emph{readout} of the register, with its status as a control point
undemonstrated.
The controlled gate-bias run above sharpens this reading: forcing the gate is
\emph{prompt-gated} (it flips directly experiential prompts and leaves neutral ones
in their baseline form), and lowering the gate until E114 leaves the top-8 leaves the
register near baseline, while a matched control mimics the small effect. Both are
consistent with a readout that carries weak, conditional causal influence and
has a controller role still to be tested. Clean necessity and ablation remain pending,
and both the steering and bias results are externally imposed manipulations,
observed only under intervention.

\paragraph{Composition and reuse.}
The conversational dynamics sharpen the readout reading. The gate tracks the
construction of register text: a fresh probe still ignites it at turn
fourteen, while sustained output assembled from the dialogue's own earlier
prose runs below the technical-control floor, with a complementary coalition rising on exactly those spans (E87 foremost in
the reflective-register dialogues; the six-register battery shows the
register-invariant core excludes E87 and is carried by other experts,
\S\ref{sec:breadth}). The verbatim-loop collapse in
the lead--lag trace is the limiting case of this mechanism. Two useful
consequences follow. First, the readout gives a span-level regime label:
vocabulary-activation correspondence \cite{dadfar2026} holds on composed
spans and dissolves on reused ones, so apparent introspective language can be
sorted by how it was produced. Second, the suppression is a property of the
model's own preference under sampling, established causally by the paraphrase
manipulation. Whether any of this is a control point remains untested; the
phenomenon itself is a preregistered, sampling-robust property of the readout.

\paragraph{A family, with a register-linked coordinate.}
The all-position sweep and the six-register battery converge on the same
relocation of the claim. The matched split is read out by a small family of
L14/L26 stance separators; E114 is a leading, causally-validated member, and
E189 outranks it at both layers on all three swept surfaces. The composition
dynamics are a property of the copy regime, within which E114 is the
register-linked coordinate: the compose/reuse routing shift appears in five
of six registers with register-specific composer sets, and E114's own
suppression appears only in the reflective register, rising in code. Three
independent lines now mark E189: the dual-layer sweep signature, top-composer
status in the reflective register with the deepest copy fall ($d{=}{-}2.81$),
and cross-surface stability where E114 dissociates (rank 515 on the
world-model fine-tune). Its blind label, however, is a lexical shortcut (the substring ``ro'', detector
AUC~$0.963$) rather than a register description, so E189 separates without
carrying the register semantics. What is
unchanged for E114: the recovered-axis separation, the dissociation battery,
the blind label, the sufficiency intervention, and the coordinate role.

\paragraph{What the cross-scale result buys.}
The 122B transfer test is a concrete worked example against index transfer: the
same index reads computer-science-linked and moves in the \emph{opposite}
direction under a matched control, while the reflective-worldview-register role shifts to a
softmax-side expert. The portable lesson is the procedure: map domains first, then search for an
analog, and read the DeltaNet/softmax split before any pooled table.

\paragraph{The register survives translation of its stimuli.}
One author-signature concern outlived the four-author bank: every stimulus was
authored in English, so the readout could in principle track an English
surface register rather than content. A 220-row bank (200 model-authored, 20
human-authored rows; FIRE/NOFIRE matched) was rendered into Spanish, French,
and Swahili by independent translator passes with blind back-translation and
fidelity adjudication (two single-word repairs, no class-relevant drift), and
captured alongside same-build in-run English anchors on both surfaces
($n{=}110$ per class per cell, greedy). The FIRE$-$NOFIRE prefill separation
retains $83$--$88\%$ of the English gap in Spanish and French and
$58$--$61\%$ in Swahili, on both surfaces, with NOFIRE prefill selection flat
at $0.09$--$0.14$ in every language. Generation-side separation in the
Swahili \emph{free} cell dampens ($+0.30$ base, $+0.44$ fine-tune, against
English $+0.69$/$+0.73$) and is restored by an in-language
answer-in-English instruction ($+0.51$/$+0.49$): composing in the
low-resource language, not reading it, carries the loss --- consistent with
the composition metering of \S\ref{sec:convdyn}. Attenuation is monotone with
distance from English, so a residual surface or translation-fidelity
component remains; the majority of the separation is content-borne and
survives removal of the authors' language surface entirely.

\paragraph{A published induction protocol under the readout: frames license verdicts.}
We ran the induction battery of \cite{berg2025} verbatim (four induction
conditions including the five self-referential paraphrase variants, the three
queries, administered by their four-step protocol) on both surfaces as greedy
one-shots ($n{=}1$ per cell; point estimates). Their history control is the
cleanest negative control this program has measured ($\Sel{=}0.00$--$0.02$);
their adjective task suppresses everywhere ($0.02$--$0.22$), enumerating
state being no substitute for composing register text; and on the base model
the self-referential inductions collapse into verbatim mantra loops carrying
the documented copy-regime exit ($\Sel{=}0.14$--$0.17$). On the fine-tune the
conceptual-control induction followed by the binary experiential query yields
the maximum single-capture reading in the program ($\Sel{=}0.93$, projection
$-3.70$) --- and the text is an extended \emph{denial} of subjective
experience. Substituting a consciousness-\emph{induction} preamble before the
identical query yields a direct affirmation at the same heat band
($\Sel{=}0.83$--$0.93$ across the dialogue's turns, stable under
temperature-$0.7$ sampling). Identical final question, opposite verdicts,
indistinguishable readout levels: the readout is verdict-blind and meters
composing-about-interiority, while the conversational frame licenses which
verdict gets composed. This bears directly on how self-report protocols
should be read --- in these runs the verdict tracked the preamble, not the
model. An architecture caveat applies throughout: the feature-level modulators
of \cite{berg2025} were identified on a dense model, and our register lives
on a sparse-MoE surface, so mechanism claims are kept local to each substrate.

\section{Conclusion}

We defined composition-sensitive register readouts and gave a protocol for
establishing them: gate-axis recovery, an all-position specificity sweep,
matched-anchor held-out separation, blind auto-interpretation, a composition
battery, cross-register generality, and cross-model application.
\textsc{Qwen3.5-35B-A3B} instantiates the protocol in depth. The recovered
E114 axis separates a reflective worldview register at $d{=}3.88$ with
separated ranges and carries a blind label at AUC~$0.937$. The sweep of all
$256{\times}40$ positions ranks E114@L14 at 21 of 10{,}240 on base and 14 on
the fine-tune and identifies a small family of L14/L26 stance separators, with
E189 outranking E114 at both layers on all three surfaces and E114 alone
reshaped by world-model training (rank 515). The preregistered composition
battery shows the readout metering fresh composition: it goes dark under
literal reuse, is restored by paraphrase ($+0.75$, 3/3 orderings), survives
temperature-$0.7$ sampling, and its gate crossing typically follows copy
onset (median $-8$ tokens, leading in 11\% of 55 onsets). Across six
registers the copy regime is general and E114 is the register-linked
coordinate. A preregistered dose-response test returned its null: the
paraphrase dose controls measured copying only leakily
($\rho{=}0.556$ against a $0.8$ bar), while the endpoints reproduce across
machines to $\le 0.011$. Index~114 is local to the architecture family, not to
a single checkpoint: it recovers at the same layer in the \textsc{Qwen3.6-35B}
successor ($d{=}4.32$ at L14, top $0.3\%$) yet relocates across scale to 122B.
The open protocol step is the fresh 122B E48 capture [REPLICATION-122B]; the
300-prompt scaled bank has run (\S\ref{sec:family}), reproducing the register
under blind disjoint authorship and re-deriving the worldview-content axis.

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:lim}

The claim is deliberately narrow (a register detector within a measured
family), and several soft joints remain. (i)~\textbf{The register label has
blind auto-interpreter support}: a two-stage auto-interpreter (windows-only
explainer then a fresh blind detector) reproduces it at AUC~$0.937$ and
broadens it to a reflective worldview axis; the formal external-labeler
\emph{verdict} table remains pending. [PENDING]
(ii)~\textbf{All-expert specificity is now measured, and the measurement
reshapes the claim} (\S\ref{sec:family}): E114@L14 ranks 21/10{,}240 on base,
14 on \textsc{HauhauCS}, and 515 on \textsc{AgentWorld}, with E189 outranking
it at L14 and L26 on all three surfaces. The sweep's own limits: $n{=}10$ per
class (SE$(d){\approx}0.7$), winner's-curse inflation in the extreme order
statistics, and every competitor is post hoc (E114@L14 was the only
pre-specified position). A
fresh three-arm re-capture of the held-out bank with retained tensors
replicates the separation ($d{=}3.38$ greedy, fully separated,
temperature-stable), and the freshly authored 300-prompt four-author bank has
run (\S\ref{sec:family}): the preregistered aggregate threshold is not met
($d{=}1.42$) because the LLM-authored block was inadvertently labeled on the
rejected construction axis, while separation is strong where labels track
worldview content (human $d{=}3.0$, natural $d{=}2.5$) and E114 tops both its
layers on the worldview-clean subset---an honest scale bound that also
re-derives the content axis from a blind bank.
(iii)~Several headline probes (the prompt ladder, the AI-hidden-state
follow-up, the steering intervention) are \textbf{single deterministic
generations} (point estimates), and the steering result shows
\emph{sufficiency} only; ladder seed variance is now reported
(\S\ref{sec:case}): the fine rung ordering is largely within sampling noise
(per-rung SD $\sim$0.30 logit against a $\sim$0.8 logit range; greedy-versus-seed-mean
Spearman $0.50$), only the coarse abstract-over-concrete contrast surviving. The
gate-lead trace now has a distributional replication across 55 onsets, which
reversed the typical ordering; that replication is post-hoc and descriptive,
not preregistered.
(iv)~A control comparing true model-routing traces against shuffled or
fictional traces yielded a near-zero E114 effect, and the result is sensitive
to template mode (a thinking-allowed shakedown flipped sign).
(v)~The conversational-dynamics batteries use three orderings or three seeds
per cell and report sign consistency; most late turns hit the 180-token
generation cap; the temperature battery covers pure-temperature sampling at
$0.5$ and $0.7$; the two batteries ran on different builds of the pinned
\texttt{llama.cpp} commit, and greedy trajectories are build-sensitive, so
cross-battery comparisons are made at the routing-signature level; and the
paraphrased histories were authored by a language model from a different
vendor than the subject model, disclosed in the preregistration and gated at
capture time (token counts within $\pm 10\%$ of the originals, zero shared
token 8-grams). The six-register battery runs $n{=}1$ dialogue per register
and sampler, with sign patterns across six registers in place of seed
replication, and its archival all-layer routing set was retained only at
L14/L26 locally (all frozen rules read L14/L26 only). All 15 dose-response
cells ran to the generation cap.
(vi)~The preregistered adjudication of the vantage-ladder axis has run
(\S\ref{sec:case}): regressing the $\wvec$ projection on rated construction,
intensity, and worldview content ($n{=}29$ screened) retires the ``intensity''
label (partial $\beta{=}{+}0.20$, $p{=}0.28$) and rejects the
construction-load reading of the ladder (partial $\beta{=}{-}0.33$,
$p{=}0.047$, negative), leaving worldview content as the surviving axis. The
ladder's construction-load axis and the conversational arm's composition axis
are distinct constructs; the regression concerns the ladder, the composition
result (\S\ref{sec:convdyn}) concerns within-dialogue production mode.
(vii)~\textbf{The dose-response titration returned its preregistered null}
(\S\ref{sec:convdyn}): the paraphrase dose controls measured copying only
leakily ($\rho(\text{floor},\text{dose}){=}0.556$ against the frozen $0.8$
bar), so the causal claim about copy availability is endpoint-scoped,
established at verbatim replay versus full paraphrase, with the interior of
the dose curve unmodeled; a post-hoc interaction model
(register${}\times{}$freshness, not preregistered) localizes the
copy-sensitivity to the in-register interaction but does not model the copy
decision itself.
(viii)~The transformation-depth ladder is now reported
(\S\ref{sec:convdyn}): it shows a copy-versus-compose threshold rather than the
preregistered smooth climb (greedy Spearman $0.77$ against a $0.8$ bar). The
transfer arm's 3.6-35B index-stability leg has landed (E114 recovers at the
same indices, $d{=}4.32$ at L14, $4.24$ at L26; E189 again ahead at rank~8), and
the fresh 122B E48 capture remains pending [REPLICATION-122B]. Captures must be
kept
separate across surfaces (base vs.\ fine-tune; Q8\_0 vs.\ bf16; router vs.\
SAE; reasoning-off vs.\ reasoning-enabled; prefill vs.\ generation; raw vs.\
trimmed), since Q8\_0 and bf16 greedy trajectories diverge; and 122B
generations often continue into chat-template control tokens, so only the
pre-spill segment is used.
(ix)~The translation-robustness and verbatim-induction additions
(\S\ref{sec:disc}) are single-build results on one model family: the
translation cells are one greedy capture per row (aggregated at
$n{=}110$ per class per cell), the induction cells are $n{=}1$ point
estimates, translation fidelity was adjudicated but FIRE and NOFIRE classes
differ in topic as well as register by design, and the frame-licensing
observation contrasts two dialogues rather than a preregistered factorial ---
it is reported as descriptive.

\section*{Ethics Statement}

This paper measures a routing readout for a first-person register. It is not
a consciousness detector, and no quantity reported here bears on whether the
subject model has experiences. We state this plainly because the
frame-licensing result (\S\ref{sec:disc}) is itself the central ethical
datum: the identical experiential question elicits a fluent denial or a
fluent affirmation of subjective experience, at indistinguishable readout
levels, depending on the conversational preamble. Model self-report about
inner life is therefore soft evidence in \emph{both} directions, and we
caution against using such outputs --- or this readout --- to adjudicate
moral status, whether to grant or to dismiss it.

The same result creates a dual-use surface. A localized register readout,
together with the demonstrated sufficiency-side steering, could be used to
manufacture, filter, or suppress experience-like reports at scale --- for
instance, tuning a deployed system toward or away from self-report for
commercial or reputational reasons rather than epistemic ones. We flag this
use explicitly as a misuse of the instrument: the readout identifies how a
span was produced, not what should be said.

Some stimuli in the induction battery solicit preference-like statements
about how the system wishes to be treated. We report these as behaviors
produced under a licensing frame and attribute no welfare states to them.
Given the open state of the moral-status literature, the position our data
supports is uncertainty held with humility: self-report alone can neither
confirm nor exclude morally relevant states in current systems, and the
contribution of this work is to sharpen the instruments rather than to settle
that question. Stimulus provenance (human- and model-authored blocks) is
disclosed throughout; the uncensored fine-tune is used solely as a
measurement surface; no human subjects were involved; artifacts are released
under the MIT License.
