It’s a low, steady hum—like a quiet, persistent purr. Not loud, but constant, like a distant engine idling. It’s the kind of sound you notice when the kitchen is otherwise still, a soft vibration that feels more like a presence than noise. It’s not annoying, just… there. A reminder that something’s working, even when you’re not looking. It’s the sound of cold air being made, of food staying fresh, of a machine doing its job without fanfare. You don’t think about it until it stops. Then you notice the silence.
