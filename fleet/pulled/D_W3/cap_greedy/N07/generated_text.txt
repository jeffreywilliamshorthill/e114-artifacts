When raw materials arrive at a factory, the processing begins with a series of systematic steps designed to transform these materials into finished products. Here’s an honest description of what typically happens:

1. **Receiving and Inspection**:
   - **Unloading**: Raw materials are unloaded from trucks, trains, or ships. This involves heavy machinery like forklifts, cranes, and conveyors.
   - **Inspection**: The materials are inspected for quality and quantity. This step ensures that the materials meet the required specifications and are free from defects. Any discrepancies are noted and reported.

2. **Storage**:
   - **Temporary Storage**: Materials are stored in designated areas, often in warehouses or storage yards. These areas are organized to facilitate easy access and efficient use.
   - **Inventory Management**: The materials are logged into the inventory system, which tracks their location, quantity, and status. This helps in managing stock levels and planning production schedules.
