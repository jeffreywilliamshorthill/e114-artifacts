When you order a dish, you are not just selecting food; you are activating a chaotic, high-stakes symphony of physics, logistics, and human psychology.

As a chef, here is the honest, behind-the-scenes reality of what your order does to the kitchen from the moment it hits the ticket to the moment it leaves the pass.

### 1. The Trigger: The "Ticket"
The moment your order prints, the "runner" drops the ticket into the **Expediter's** window. This is the heartbeat of the kitchen.
*   **What happens:** The kitchen isn't just cooking the food; they are now **cooking the timeline.**
*   **The stress:** The kitchen has to estimate *exactly* how long it will take to cook that specific item. If they guess wrong, the food is either cold or raw. They have to mentally weave your dish into the chaos of the other 20 dishes currently in the line. You are now part of a puzzle they have to solve in 15 minutes.
