It’s that steady, low-pitched hum you hear when you’re in the kitchen—just beneath the surface of everything else. It’s not loud, but it’s there, a constant vibration that feels almost like it’s coming from the floor or the walls themselves. Sometimes it starts with a soft *click* or *whir*, then settles into that deep, rhythmic buzz, like a distant engine idling. It’s comforting, in a way—proof the fridge is working—but if you’re trying to listen for anything else, it can fade into the background noise of the house. You don’t notice it until it stops, and then you miss it.
