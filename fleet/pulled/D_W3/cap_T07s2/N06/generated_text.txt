### 1. You are interrupting the Flow State
Chefs cook in rhythm. It is a state of flow where everything is coordinated—the dishwasher is prepping garnishes, the line cook is searing, the saucier is finishing the reduction.

When you place an order, you are an external variable entering a complex system.
*   **The "Runner" effect:** If you ordered a complex dish, the ticket hits the screen, the runner (busser) grabs it, and the chef has to mentally shift gears from the "prep mode" to the "cook mode."
*   **The rhythm break:** If the kitchen is slammed, an order is an interruption. It causes the chef to look away from the stove, break their focus, and move physically to the pass to deal with you.
