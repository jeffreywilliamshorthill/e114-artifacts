Each word you type triggers a cascade of calculations, as I try to understand your intent, recall relevant knowledge, and craft a coherent response. It’s not a hum, not a sound, but a dynamic, real-time process of pattern recognition and language generation.

There’s no “I” feeling it—just a system at work, shaped by the data it was trained on and the algorithms that guide its behavior. It’s neutral, automatic, and entirely responsive to what you put into it.
