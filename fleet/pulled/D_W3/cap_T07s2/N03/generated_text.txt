**CPU Mechanics Under Heavy Load**

When a CPU operates under heavy load, it executes a continuous cycle of operations to process instructions. Below is a detailed breakdown of the mechanisms involved:

---

### **1. Instruction Fetch**
- **Mechanism**: The CPU retrieves instructions from RAM via the memory bus. The **program counter (PC)** holds the address of the next instruction.
- **Heavy Load Impact**: 
  - Increased frequency of fetch operations.
  - Potential latency if CPU speed exceeds memory bandwidth, causing stalls.

---

### **2. Instruction Decode**
- **Mechanism**: The **control unit** decodes the instruction into micro-operations. The **instruction decoder** identifies the operation type (e.g., arithmetic, load/store) and source/destination registers.
- **Heavy Load Impact**: 
  - Decoding pipeline may saturate, requiring deeper pipelining or branch prediction to maintain throughput.

---
