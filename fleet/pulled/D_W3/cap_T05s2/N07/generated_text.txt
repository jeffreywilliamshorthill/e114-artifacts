When raw materials arrive at a factory, the processing begins with a series of carefully orchestrated steps to ensure efficiency, quality, and safety. Here’s an honest breakdown of what typically happens:

---

### 1. **Receiving and Inspection**
   - **Unloading:** Raw materials are unloaded from trucks, ships, or trains using cranes, forklifts, or conveyor systems.
   - **Documentation Check:** The materials are matched against purchase orders and shipping manifests to verify quantities and types.
   - **Quality Inspection:** Samples may be taken for testing to ensure the materials meet specifications (e.g., purity, dimensions, strength). Defective or substandard materials are rejected or quarantined.

---

### 2. **Storage and Inventory Management**
   - **Temporary Holding:** Materials are moved to designated storage areas, which may include warehouses, silos, tanks, or racks.
   - **Inventory Tracking:** Barcodes, RFID tags, or manual logs are used to track inventory levels and locations. This helps prevent shortages or overstocking.
   - **Environmental Controls:** Some materials require specific conditions (e.g., temperature, humidity) to maintain quality.
