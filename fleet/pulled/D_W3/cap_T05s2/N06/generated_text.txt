Ordering a dish is not just a transaction; it is a command that sends a shockwave through the entire kitchen. It is a domino effect that starts with you and ends with you, but in between, it disrupts the delicate, high-stakes equilibrium of the kitchen brigade.

Here is the honest experience of what happens when you place an order, broken down by the chaos it creates.

### 1. The "Ticket" and the Panic (The Front of House)
When you order, the server doesn't just write it down; they trigger a timer. The ticket hits the screen or the printer.
*   **The Reality:** The kitchen is already running at 100% capacity. They are likely 15 to 30 minutes behind schedule. Your order is now competing with the orders of the table next to you, the table in the corner, and the "rush" orders that are already on the line.
