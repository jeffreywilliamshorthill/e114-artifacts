The refrigerator motor hums with a low, steady drone, like a distant engine idling. It’s a deep, rhythmic thrum that vibrates faintly through the floorboards, almost imperceptible unless you’re standing right next to it. Occasionally, it shifts pitch—rising slightly as the compressor kicks on, then settling back into its baseline growl. There’s a subtle mechanical edge to it, a metallic rasp that hints at the gears and coils working inside. It’s not loud, but it’s constant, a quiet backdrop to the kitchen’s silence, like the fridge is breathing. If you listen closely, the hum feels alive, a reminder of the unseen machinery keeping things cold.
