3. **Communication** – While purring can signal contentment, it’s also used to solicit attention (e.g., when hungry or seeking affection). The cat may notice how the hum draws human attention or elicits a response.  

4. **Bonding** – When purring while being petted, the cat likely feels a sense of connection and trust. The hum reinforces social bonds with their human or other cats.
