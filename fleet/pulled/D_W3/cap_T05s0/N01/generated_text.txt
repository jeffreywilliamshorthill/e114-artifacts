It’s a low, steady hum, like a distant engine idling. You don’t notice it at first—just a faint vibration in the air, almost like a soft purr. Then, when you’re quiet, it settles into your bones. It’s not loud, but it’s there, a constant companion in the background. Sometimes it dips, like a breath held too long, then rises again, steady and unyielding. It’s the sound of something working, something keeping things cold, something that never stops. It’s not annoying, not exactly. It’s just… there. Like the house breathing.
