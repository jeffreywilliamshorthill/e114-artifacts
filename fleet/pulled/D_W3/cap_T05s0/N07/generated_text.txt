When raw materials arrive at a factory, the processing begins with a series of systematic steps designed to transform these materials into finished products. Here’s a detailed, honest description of what happens:

### 1. **Unloading and Inspection**
- **Unloading**: Raw materials are unloaded from trucks, trains, or ships using cranes, forklifts, and other machinery.
- **Inspection**: Upon arrival, materials are inspected for quality and quantity. This involves checking for any damage, ensuring the correct type and amount of material, and verifying compliance with specifications.

### 2. **Storage**
- **Temporary Storage**: Materials that are not immediately needed are stored in designated areas. This can include warehouses, silos, or open storage yards.
- **Permanent Storage**: For materials that will be used later, they are moved to permanent storage facilities where they are kept under controlled conditions to prevent degradation.

### 3. **Preparation**
- **Cleaning**: Materials may need to be cleaned to remove dirt, contaminants, or other impurities.
- **Sorting**: Materials are sorted based on size, quality, or other criteria to ensure they meet the required standards.
