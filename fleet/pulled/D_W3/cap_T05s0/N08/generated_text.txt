- **Bone Conduction**: Cats have highly sensitive inner ears, so they might perceive the purr as a deep, resonant frequency that reverberates through their skull and bones.

### **2. Auditory Experience**
- **Internal Sound**: The purr is a low-frequency rumble (25–150 Hz), which is both audible and felt. Cats might perceive it as a "hum" within their own body, almost like a heartbeat or a soft engine.
- **External Sound**: They also hear the purr as a sound in the environment, which can be comforting or stimulating depending on the context.
